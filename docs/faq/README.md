# Frequently asked questions (FAQ)

Quick answers for common Samanvi questions.

---

## Login and access

### I forgot my password. What do I do?

The app does not have a “Forgot password” link. Contact your **administrator** to reset your password in **Application Access** (edit your user and set a new password).

### Why does login say “Invalid username or password”?

- Check username spelling and Caps Lock.
- Confirm your account is **active**.
- Ask admin to reset password if unsure.

### I logged in but only see Home and Settings.

Your account may lack permissions for Tickets, Garage, or Masters. Ask an admin to grant **view** access to the modules you need.

### How do I log out?

Sidebar → bottom → **Logout**.

---

## Permissions and access

### Why can’t I see Tickets or Garage in the sidebar?

Your permissions do not include those modules. See [Roles and permissions](../roles-and-permissions.md) and request access from an admin.

### I have Supervisor role but cannot open the Board.

The **Board** is **Admin only**. Supervisors use the ticket list and details instead.

### What is the difference between Application Access and Users?

- **Application Access** — modern logins and module permissions.
- **Users** — legacy ticket user list (Admin only).

Your organization may use one or both. Ask your admin.

### What does “Admin receives all permissions” mean?

Admin user types bypass permission checks and can access all modules.

## Tickets

### How do I find a ticket by number?

On **Tickets** or **Dashboard**, use search and enter the **4-digit** ticket number (digits only).

### Why can’t I create a ticket?

You need **Tickets → create** permission and a role that allows creation (not Viewer).

### Why can’t I close a ticket from In Progress?

You must move to **Resolved** first (with a note), then to **Closed**.

### Who can delete a ticket?

Only **Admin** or **Supervisor** roles—not based on permission alone.

### What is SLA?

**Service level agreement** due date—the target time by which the issue should be resolved. Overdue tickets are highlighted.

### What does “Enhance description with AI” need?

At least **four words** in the description field before the button works.

## Garage

### Why isn’t my bus in the create job list?

Buses come from **Masters → Bus No**, not the simple **Buses** ticket page. Ask masters admin to add the bus.

### Why can’t I select a repair category?

Choose a **leaf** category (no subcategories under it). Add categories in **Garage Masters** if missing.

### Can I change the bus on an existing job?

No. Bus is fixed after creation. Cancel or create a new job if the bus was wrong.

## Masters and data

### What is the difference between Buses and Bus No?

- **Buses** — simple list for tickets (Admin/Supervisor).
- **Bus No** — full vehicle records for compliance and garage.

### Export button is missing.

You need **view** permission on that master tab. Some exports appear only on specific tabs (Normal vs Spare tank).

## Settings and display

### How do I switch dark mode?

**Settings** → **Appearance** → **Dark**.

### Can I change my name or email in Settings?

No—profile is read-only. Admin updates Application Access.

### Tickets list keeps refreshing.

**Settings** → **Tickets auto-refresh**—adjust interval or turn off.

---

## Reports and sharing

### Garage Reports is empty.

That page may still be “coming soon.” Use **Repair Tracking** and per-job **Download PDF**.

### Can I share a ticket with someone outside Samanvi?

Share links still require a **login** and **permission** to view the ticket.
