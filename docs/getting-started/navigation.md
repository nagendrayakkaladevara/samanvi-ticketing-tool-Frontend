# Navigation overview

## The main layout

After you sign in, most screens share the same layout:

| Area | Location | Purpose |
|------|----------|---------|
| **Sidebar** | Left | Go to Home, Masters, Garage, Application Access, Settings |
| **Main content** | Center/right | Lists, forms, and details for the page you opened |
| **Header** (on some pages) | Top of content | Page title, back button, primary actions |

On **phones**, the sidebar may hide behind a menu button—tap it to open navigation, then tap a link; the menu closes automatically.

---

## Sidebar sections

### Logo and Home

- Click the **Samanvi logo** or **Home** to return to the welcome screen.

### Masters (expandable)

Click **Masters** to expand:

| Link | Opens |
|------|--------|
| **Service For** | Service-type lookup values |
| **Bus No** | Bus registry and spare tanks |
| **Service No** | Route and service number definitions |
| **Employees** | Drivers, helpers, and office staff |

You only see **Masters** if your account has permission for at least one of these areas.

### Garage (expandable)

Click **Garage** to expand:

| Link | Opens |
|------|--------|
| **Create Repair Job** | New repair form |
| **Repair Tracking** | List of repair jobs |
| **Reports** | Garage reports (content may be limited) |
| **Garage Masters** | Repair categories and spare parts |

### Other navigation items

Depending on your permissions, you may also see:

| Item | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| **Application Access** | Manage user accounts |
| **Voice app access** | Opens an external voice dashboard (new browser tab) |
| **Settings** | Your profile and preferences |

Some links (Dashboard, Tickets, Board, Users, Buses) may be available via **direct URL** or admin configuration even when not shown in the sidebar—ask your admin if you need them.

### Footer

- **Version** — e.g. v3.0.0
- **Logout** — ends your session

---

## Collapsing the sidebar

On desktop, you can **collapse** the sidebar to icons only:

- Hover or click the edge control (**sidebar rail**) to expand or collapse.
- Collapsed mode still shows tooltips when you hover icons.

---

## Breadcrumbs and back buttons

Many forms include:

- **Back to tickets** / **Back to repair tracking** — returns to the list without saving
- **Cancel** — leaves the form (confirm if you have unsaved work)

---

## Direct links and sharing

- **Tickets** and **repair jobs** can be **shared** via a link from the list or detail page (copy link / share action).
- Opening a shared link requires you to be **logged in** with permission to view that record.

---

## Where am I?

The browser **address bar** shows the area:

| Path pattern | Area |
|--------------|------|
| `/` | Home |
| `/tickets` | Ticket list |
| `/tickets/create` | New ticket |
| `/tickets/...` | Ticket details |
| `/dashboard` | Dashboard |
| `/garage/...` | Garage |
| `/masters/...` | Masters |
| `/application-access` | User accounts |
| `/settings` | Settings |
