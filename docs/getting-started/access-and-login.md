# Access and login

## How to access the application

Samanvi runs in a **web browser** (Chrome, Edge, Safari, or Firefox recommended).

1. Open the URL your organization gave you (for example, your company’s Samanvi dashboard link).
2. You should see the **login** screen titled **Samanvi Internal Tool**.
3. Bookmark the page on computers you use regularly.

> **Tip:** On mobile, you can install the app to your home screen from **Settings → Install app** (if your device supports it).

---

## Login instructions

### Step 1: Open the login page

If you are not signed in, the app sends you to `/login` automatically.

### Step 2: Enter your credentials

| Field | Rules |
|-------|--------|
| **Username** | At least 3 characters, up to 64 |
| **Password** | At least 8 characters, up to 128 |

### Step 3: Sign in

1. Type your **username**.
2. Type your **password** (use the show/hide control if you need to check spelling).
3. Click **Login**.

On success, you are taken to your **home** screen or the first area you are allowed to use (often Dashboard or Tickets).

### If login fails

- Read the message on screen—it usually means the username or password is wrong.
- Check that **Caps Lock** is off.
- Try again after a moment if the network was slow.

---

## Password reset

**There is no “Forgot password” link in the app today.**

To get a new password:

1. Contact your **administrator** or whoever manages **Application Access**.
2. They can set a new password when **creating** or **editing** your account.

**Do not share** your password by phone or chat. Ask the admin to set a temporary password and change it on your next login if your organization allows that.

---

## Signing out

1. Open the **sidebar** (left side of the screen).
2. Scroll to the bottom.
3. Click **Logout**.

Always log out on shared computers.

---

## Account status

Your account must be **active** to sign in. If you see errors even with the correct password, your account may be **inactive**—contact your administrator.

---

## Security recommendations

- Use a **strong, unique** password.
- Do not share your login with colleagues.
- Log out when you leave a shared device.
- Report suspicious activity to your admin immediately.
