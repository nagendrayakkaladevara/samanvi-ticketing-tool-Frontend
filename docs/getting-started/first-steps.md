# First steps after login

Follow this checklist the first time you use Samanvi.

---

## 1. Confirm you reached the right place

After login you should see either:

- The **Home** welcome screen with your name and role, or
- **Dashboard**, **Tickets**, or **Application Access**—depending on what your account can access first.

If you see a blank page or “not found,” tell your administrator your permissions may be incomplete.

---

## 2. Open Settings and review your profile

1. Click **Settings** in the sidebar.
2. Review your **name**, **username**, **email**, and **role**.
3. Confirm **Account active** shows as active.

Profile fields are **read-only** here—contact an admin to change your name or role.

---

## 3. Choose your theme (optional)

In **Settings → Appearance**:

1. Select **Light** for daytime use, or **Dark** for less glare.
2. Your choice is saved on this device.

---

## 4. Set useful preferences (optional)

Still in **Settings**, you may see:

| Section | What it does |
|---------|----------------|
| **Reporting period default** | Default date range for dashboard-style views |
| **Tickets auto-refresh** | How often the ticket list refreshes automatically |
| **Install app** | Add Samanvi to your phone home screen |

Adjust these to match how you work.

---

## 5. Explore your allowed areas

Walk through each sidebar section you can see:

| If you see… | Try this first |
|-------------|----------------|
| **Tickets** (or ask admin for link) | Open the list; open one ticket to see details |
| **Masters → Bus No** | Search for a bus you know |
| **Garage → Repair Tracking** | View recent repair jobs |
| **Application Access** | Open the user list (if you manage accounts) |

Do not create or edit production records until your trainer confirms you are ready.

---

## 6. Learn one complete workflow

Pick the workflow that matches your job:

| Your job | Start here |
|----------|------------|
| Report bus issues | [Create a ticket](../workflows/creating-a-ticket.md) |
| Fix or track issues | [Manage ticket status](../workflows/managing-ticket-status.md) |
| Garage staff | [Create a repair job](../workflows/creating-repair-job.md) |
| IT / admin | [Manage application users](../workflows/user-management.md) |

---

## 7. Know how to get help

- **Missing menu?** → [Roles and permissions](../roles-and-permissions.md)
- **Cannot save a form?** → Check required fields; see [FAQ](../faq/README.md)
- **Wrong password?** → Ask admin (no self-service reset)

---

## Dashboard quick orientation (admins and ticket viewers)

If you have access to **Dashboard** (`/dashboard`):

- **Summary cards** show totals: open, unassigned, in progress, overdue, and more.
- **Reporting period** dropdown changes the time window (Today, 7 days, 30 days, etc.).
- Click a card or row to open a **filtered ticket list**.
- Use **Create Ticket** or **View All Tickets** for common actions.

Full detail: [Dashboard feature guide](../features/dashboard.md).

---

## You are ready

Once you can sign in, find your main screen, and complete one practice task, you are ready for daily use. Keep this manual bookmarked for step-by-step help.
