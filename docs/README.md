# Samanvi Internal Tool — User Manual

Welcome to the **Samanvi Internal Tool**. This guide helps you use the application day to day—without technical jargon.

Use the sections below like a help center: start with **Getting Started**, then open the guide for the area you work in.

---

## What is Samanvi?

Samanvi is an internal workspace for Samanvi employees. It brings together:

- **Tickets** — Report and track fleet issues (breakdowns, delays, safety concerns, and more).
- **Garage** — Log and follow bus repair jobs, spare parts, and repair categories.
- **Masters** — Keep reference data accurate (buses, routes, drivers, helpers, office staff).
- **Application Access** — Create and manage who can sign in and what they can do.

The app is designed for operations teams, supervisors, garage staff, and administrators.

---

## Who uses Samanvi?

| User type | Typical work |
|-----------|----------------|
| **Admin** | Full access: board view, user management, tickets, garage, masters |
| **Supervisor** | Tickets, buses, team oversight; same ticket powers as admin in many cases |
| **Worker** | Update tickets assigned to them; garage jobs if permitted |
| **Viewer** | Read tickets only; cannot change status or comment |
| **Account-specific roles** | Chairman, Accountant, Collection Agent, and others—access depends on permissions set for your account |

> **Note:** What you see in the sidebar depends on your **permissions**, not only your role label. If a menu item is missing, your account may not have access to that area.

## Quick tips

- Use the **sidebar** on the left to open any area you have access to. **Masters** and **Garage** expand to show sub-pages.
- After login, you may land on **Home**, **Dashboard**, **Tickets**, or **Application Access**—depending on what your account can use.
- Required form fields are marked clearly. If submit fails, check for red error messages under fields.
- **Logout** is at the bottom of the sidebar.

1. Check the [FAQ](faq/README.md) and [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting/README.md) guides.
